# Feature Research

## Core Feature Areas

### Platform Foundation

- Secure operator sign-in with internal role control
- Connector health and sync status
- Audit log for workflow runs and admin actions
- Shared entity model for users, devices, systems, sites, groups, and documents

### Asset Health Dashboard

- Device inventory with owner, location, age, OS, encryption, AV, patch state, and last check-in
- Risk scoring and alerting for stale, unhealthy, or aging assets
- Drill-down views for per-device history and recommended actions

### Onboarding / Offboarding Automator

- Reusable templates by role or department
- Checklist-driven workflow execution
- License, group, and access assignment suggestions
- Evidence trail of completed and skipped steps

### Network Visibility Lite

- Site-aware network inventory covering WAN links, firewalls, switches, APs, and core DHCP or VPN services
- Lightweight mapper showing site, WAN, and LAN relationships
- Triage queue for offline network devices, stale telemetry, and topology gaps
- Confidence labels separating confirmed topology from inferred relationships

### Backup Confidence Dashboard

- Protected-system inventory
- Backup freshness view
- Restore-test tracking
- Gap reporting for systems without backup evidence

### Documentation Assistant

- Searchable SOPs, contacts, vendors, firewall notes, diagrams, and recovery steps
- Tagging and relationship links between documents and operational entities
- Change history and review prompts for aging documents

## Deferred Feature Areas

- Maintenance scheduler
- Ticket intake and triage
- Printer/share mapping
- License utilization watcher

## Product Shape

The best version of this product behaves like a compact internal operations hub: a morning dashboard, a guided workflow runner, a lightweight network map, and a knowledge base in one place.
